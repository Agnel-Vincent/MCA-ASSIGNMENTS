Seekpath

One queue of disk requests, served two ways.

Across is the cylinder number. Down is time, one row per request served. Each slanted line is one seek, with its length printed beside it.

Step, seek to, distance moved, total so far

    FCFS

    Serves requests in the order they arrived. No request is skipped, so none waits forever, but the head can swing across the disk and back again and again.

    SSTF

    From wherever the head is, serve the closest waiting request next. Total travel is usually far shorter. The cost is starvation: a distant request can wait indefinitely while nearer ones keep arriving.

    Total seek distance

    The sum of |next − current| over every move, starting from the head’s first position. Fewer cylinders crossed means less time spent moving the arm.
